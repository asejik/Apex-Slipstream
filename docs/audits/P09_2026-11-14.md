## 1. System Architecture & Module Boundaries
- **Core Loop & Decoupling:** The simulation loop runs on a strict fixed-timestep accumulator ($dt = 1/60\text{ s} \approx 0.01667\text{ s}$), cleanly decoupled from rendering framerate variable deltas.
- **Subsystem Separation:**
  - `src/engine/VehiclePhysics.ts`: Handles vehicle acceleration, steering angle, drift physics, tire grip coefficients, and aerodynamic drag.
  - `src/track/TrackSpline.ts` & `TrackGeometryGenerator.ts`: Procedural 3D track extrusion with Catmull-Rom splines, elevation banking, and surface zones.
  - `src/ai/AIDriverManager.ts`: Controls 19 autonomous AI racers with randomized driver affinities, spline tracking, lateral obstacle avoidance, and clean grid slots.
  - `src/engine/CollisionManager.ts`: OBB/radial vehicle-to-vehicle collision detection with bounce impulses and spinout triggers.
  - `src/engine/ChampionshipEngine.ts` & `QualifyingManager.ts`: Official 1st–15th FIA-style points structure, fastest lap bonus, clean driving bonus, and 1-Lap Flying Shootout vs. Sector Sprint qualifying.
  - `src/storage/GhostStorage.ts` & `src/replay/`: 30Hz binary `Float32Array` recording, IndexedDB persistence, shortest-arc angular interpolation, and holographic playback.
  - `src/ui/TouchController.ts` & `HUDController.ts`: Mobile touch overlay and Direction A cyber-telemetry HUD.

---

## 2. Memory Hygiene & Performance
- **Zero Allocations in Hot Loop:**
  - Zero `new THREE.Vector3()` or closure instantiations inside `update()` or `render()` ticks.
  - Scratch vectors and matrix variables are instantiated once in constructor scopes and reused via `.set()` or `.copy()`.
  - Ghost telemetry sampling uses a pre-allocated flat `Float32Array` buffer, eliminating GC pauses during gameplay.
- **Draw Call & Rendering Efficiency:**
  - Low-poly procedural geometry with unlit/flat materials.
  - Ground plane and horizon perspective grid use single pre-allocated meshes.
  - Smooth 60 FPS verified on both desktop and mobile viewports.

## 3. Product Quality & UX
- **Arcade Feel & Controls:**
  - Responsive keyboard, gamepad (Gamepad API), and mobile multi-touch virtual controls.
  - Responsive visual feedback for slipstream wake cones, tire grip readouts, and live time deltas.
- **Fail-Safe Persistence:**
  - Season state persists across browser reloads via `localStorage`.
  - Replays persist in `IndexedDB` with an automatic in-memory fallback for private/incognito browsing.
- **Accessibility & Mobile Ergonomics:**
  - Touch controls automatically adapt with `touch-action: none` and `{ passive: false }`.
  - Orientation lock overlay prompts users when held in portrait mode.
  - High-contrast HUD indicators (cyan, lime, amber, crimson) maintain WCAG 2.2 AA contrast against the dark background (`#06080e`).
