### P09-R01 (MEDIUM): pause menu "Qualifying" starts qualifying inside a Quick Race or Time Trial
- **Where:**
  - `index.html:1145`: `<button class="menu-item" id="btn-qualify-toggle">` is always shown. `src/ui/PauseMenu.ts` `show()` never hides it.
  - `src/main.ts:572-607` `promptQualifyingSession()` sets `isQualifying = true` but never clears `this.quickRace` or `this.timeTrial`.
  - `loadTrack` (`main.ts:438-442`) then prefers the Quick Race or Time Trial track:
    ```ts
    const trackData = this.timeTrial ? timeTrialTrack(this.timeTrial)
      : this.quickRace ? quickRaceTrack(this.quickRace) : (ALL_TRACKS[index] ?? ALL_TRACKS[0]!);
    ```
  - `onQualifyingCompleted` (`main.ts:1068-1087`) builds benchmarks from `seasonManager.currentTrack` and goes to `'grid'`, with the Quick Race or Time Trial still set.
- **Evidence:** Playwright on the dev server, game state read through the dev-only `__game` handle. The end of qualifying was called directly; everything before it went through real clicks.
  ```
  probe A (quick race running): quickRace={trackIndex:3}, trackOnScreen "Alpine Ridge Pass", seasonTrack "Coastal Highway Circuit"
  probe B (qualifying modal title): Coastal Highway Circuit
  probe C (after Start qualifying): isQualifying:true, quickRace still {trackIndex:3}, loadedTrackIndex:0, trackOnScreen "Alpine Ridge Pass", aiVisible:false
  probe D (after qualifying completed): screen "grid", quickRace still set, loadedTrackIndex:0, trackOnScreen "Alpine Ridge Pass"; tyre screen "Quick race · Alpine Ridge Pass"
  probe2 (time trial): after qualifying → screen "grid", timeTrial still set, aiVisible:true; tyre screen shows "Round 1 of 8 · Coastal Highway Circuit · Forecast RAIN" for a dry Time Trial
  ```
- **Impact:**
  - The modal names one track, the qualifying lap runs on another, and the grid comes from a third reference.
  - From a Time Trial you reach a "Round 1 of 8" grid with visible AI that never moves, because `isSolo` is still true.
  - No season data is corrupted. The race stays a Quick Race or Time Trial, so `recordRaceResult` is never called. Hence MEDIUM, not HIGH.
- **Fix (minimal):** pass `canQualify: this.isChampionshipRace || this.isPractice` into `PauseMenu.show` and hide the button when false. A guard at the top of `promptQualifyingSession` is the safety net.
- **Proper fix:** the single session value in P09-R02.
- **Same pattern:** in `cycleTrack`, `restartRace`, `startQuickRace` (does not clear `timeTrial`) and `togglePracticeWeather`, each function clears a different subset of the four flags by hand.
- **Verify yourself:** Quick Race on Alpine, then Pause, then Qualifying. The modal says "Coastal Highway Circuit" while you drive Alpine.

  1. `src/app/Session.ts`: one discriminated union replacing the four flags:

  5. `src/app/MenuController.ts`: hub, garage, records, championships, settings and keys glue. The Esc chain becomes a list of closable screens.
  6. `main.ts` keeps composition and the loop outline: about 250 lines.
- **Precondition:** P09-R03.
