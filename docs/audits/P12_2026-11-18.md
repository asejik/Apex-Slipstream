## 4. Detail

**P12-R01 (LOW): the car model.**

First visit, from the browser's resource timing:
```
page          11.0 KB
243.4 KB /models/race-car.glb
209.3 KB /assets/index-opqJgZAL.js        (Brotli)
 22.2 KB /assets/barlow-condensed-latin-800-normal-BKzMuPgK.woff2
 22.2 KB /assets/barlow-condensed-latin-700-normal-v1xN8_Wq.woff2
 22.1 KB /assets/barlow-condensed-latin-600-normal-DepVgxBB.woff2
  4.5 KB /assets/index-Cn9qBbZK.css
TOTAL 534.8 KB
```

- **No compression:** the model arrives at its full size (`model/gltf-binary` gets no Brotli from Cloudflare).
- **No long cache:** `Cache-Control: public, max-age=0, must-revalidate` means a check on every online visit. After the first visit the service worker serves it, so in practice this only costs the first load.
- **Fix:** compress the geometry with meshopt or Draco (three.js loaders exist), aiming for about 60–100 KB. Optionally give `/models/*` a week-long cache in `public/_headers`. A new decoder may need a new dependency, so this needs your OK.
- **Verify:** the first-visit total drops by about 150 KB.

## 5. CHECKED AND PASSED

**Section 1: payload**
- **First visit:** 535 KB in all.
- **Game code:** one 796 KB JS bundle, sent Brotli-compressed at 209 KB. three.js is most of it.
- **Fonts:** only the three weights the hub uses load (`woff2`, 22 KB each).
- **No other assets:** there are no images apart from the icons, and no audio files (sound is synthesised with Web Audio).

**Section 4: caching** (`curl -D -` on each file):

| File | Cache-Control |
|---|---|
| `/assets/*` (hashed names) | `public, max-age=31536000, immutable` |

| `/`, `/sw.js` | `max-age=0, must-revalidate` (right: they must update) |

- **Repeat visits** load the game from the service worker's cache (#77), and offline as well.
