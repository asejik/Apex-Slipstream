**P15-09 measurement.** The race loop still makes about **10 KB of garbage per frame**.
- **Method:**
  - Quick Race at Coastal in rain, past the lights.
  - `renderer.render` stubbed, `gameLoop` stepped by hand.
  - `performance.memory.usedJSHeapSize` sampled every 120 frames.
- **Result:** the heap climbed by about 8 MB per 600 frames before each garbage collection, about 13 KB per frame.
- **The one clear cause:** stubbing `MiniMap.update` cut the median from about 10.0 KB to about 3.9 KB per frame. It redraws every dot each frame and reads `canvas.clientWidth`, which also forces a layout.
- **Not pinned down:** the rest of the measurements were too noisy to blame anything else. The fix task should use DevTools allocation sampling.
- Breaks the AGENTS.md hot-loop rule.

**P15-10 measurement.** `renderer.info.memory.geometries` read after each `loadTrack`:
- Six different tracks, then back to the hub, then six reloads of the same track.
- Values: 35, 46, 57, 68, 79, 90, 101, 112, 123, 134, 145, 156, 167.
- That is **+11 geometries per load**, never freed. Every race start, restart and practice track leaks GPU memory.

**P15-14.**

- It does not precache the hashed JS bundle, CSS or fonts. The page that installs the service worker loads them before it controls the page, so they never reach the cache.
- Result: the first offline visit has no code. Not run offline here, because the pane cannot go offline.

**P15-16 detail.**
- **Where it happens:** `showRaceResults` records the finale with `seasonManager.recordRaceResult` (sets `seasonCompleted`, leaves `currentRaceIndex` on the last round) and `career.recordSeason(trophy)`. Only tapping through the podium calls `resetSeason()`. Nothing in `main.ts` reads `isSeasonCompleted`.
- **How it goes wrong:** you close or reload the game on the results or podium screen. The hub then offers the final round again, and racing it adds its points a second time. Since R9 it also adds a second trophy and season to the career.

| R10-02 | LOW | Finished AI cars drive through each other after the flag | `AIDriverManager` marks a car inactive for traffic once it has a finish time (`traffic.active[i] = car.isParked \|\| car.finishTime !== null ? 0 : 1`). **R10-01 detail.**
- **Where:** `VehiclePhysics.step` applies `effectiveDrag` (surface drag × tyre drag, `getTireDragMultiplier`) only when `throttle === 0 && brake === 0`. Under throttle, only off-road drag above 1.2 applies.
- **Effect:** a compound's `dryDragMultiplier` changes nothing while you accelerate. In practice tyre choice works through grip alone. Tyre choice still matters (R7 checks), but one of its two levers is mostly dead.
